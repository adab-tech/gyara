Hausa tone review

Fifty words with proposed tones. Press play to hear each one with its tones, then judge the pattern.

How to judge a word
  1. Press Hear the proposal to play the word with the proposed tones. Where Newman differs, press the play button beside Newman's pattern to compare.
  2. Judge the pitch only. H is high, L is low, F is a fall inside one syllable. The voice is a basic synthetic one, so its vowels and consonants are rough.
  3. Press Right, Wrong or Not sure. If Newman's pattern is the right one, press This one is right beside it. Otherwise mark Wrong, tap the letters under the syllables to set the pattern, and press Hear my pattern to check it.

About the voice: it is not the Murya voice. Murya reads text without tone marks, so it could not test these labels. This is eSpeak NG with Swahili settings, with each syllable's pitch set from the pattern: H at about 129 Hz, L at about 88 Hz, and F gliding from one to the other. Long vowels in the Wiktionary spelling are played long.

Assumptions to check: a vowel with no tone mark counts as high, and a macron only marks length. If that is wrong for a word, mark it Wrong and say so in the note. Blind hides the proposals until you have set your own pattern, so they cannot nudge your answer.
